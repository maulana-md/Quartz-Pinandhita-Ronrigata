## Commentary

This translation was commissioned by Anonymous. Many thanks!

This is a silly porn about Rin, Sakura, and Shinji. You don't need to know much about them to follow or jerk off to this.

Honestly, I'm not much of a fan of Ankoman's works. I'm honored that two people want us to translate Ankoman's works, but reflecting on how we translated this and [[22.25 Saoyaku Yamato Takeru ga Onna Eirei-tachi to Ecchi Shimakuru Hon|Yamato Takeru's book]], I think it's best in the future to leave it to other TL group who is actually deep in Fate / F/GO and/or cares more about the artist and their works.
